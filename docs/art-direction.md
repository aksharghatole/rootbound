# Rootbound visual direction

A quiet, mystical woodland survival game. The forest should feel alive, but interactions must remain legible and accessible on Android.

## Inspiration and implementation
- [Pinterest: forest and earthy palettes](https://guide.pinterest.com/design/green-and-brown-color-scheme-name/) — deep green anchors, muted moss, bark brown and warm gold.
- [Pinterest: comfort palettes](https://guide.pinterest.com/design/comfort-color-hex-codes/) — restrained accents and gentle contrast.
- [Pinterest: backyard botanist trend](https://newsroom.pinterest.com/news/2026-pinterest-hobbies-trend-report/) — botanical silhouettes and close observation of plants.

These references inform the mood only. No images from Pinterest are copied into the repository. The title-screen SVG is original vector artwork constructed from geometric shapes and paths.

## UI principles
- A clear focal tree, layered forest silhouettes and subtle light.
- Cream text on dark backgrounds; gold for primary actions.
- Keep decorative elements outside hit targets, support safe-area margins.
- Prefer lightweight vector assets and event-driven animation to heavy effects.

## Device review
Inspect the title screen on a portrait phone at 720x1280-equivalent logical size and a taller notched phone; verify button focus, text legibility, artwork cropping, save/continue and touch targets.
